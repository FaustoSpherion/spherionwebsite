Applications

Where a sphere-first representation may matter.

Spherion is most relevant where structure, curvature, uncertainty, viewpoint, and multiple scales are not secondary complications, but central features of the problem.

Spatial localization

Represent location as nested spherical inclusion with explicit ambiguity rather than as a single coordinate tuple.

Robotics

Use adjacency and observer-relative motion to reason about orientation, navigation, and changing frames.

Artificial intelligence

Explore non-grid representations for structured data, uncertainty propagation, and multi-resolution inference.

Quantum foundations

Study probabilistic inclusion, collapse, and frame dependence through a sphere-first conceptual language.

Geospatial systems

Compare spherical zones with standard coordinate-heavy or tile-heavy approaches to Earth-scale data.

Education

Teach geometry from the whole to the part: sphere, zones, relationships, motion, and refinement.

Responsible framing

The site should describe applications as research directions, not as completed replacements for existing systems. This strengthens credibility and gives collaborators clear problems to test.
